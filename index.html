The opportunity

The work is repetitive.
The environment isn’t.

Overhead MEP rough-in is high-value, high-volume and chronically constrained by skilled labor. It is also performed above shoulder height, around other trades, in ceilings that rarely match the model perfectly.

That combination has kept the hardest part of MEP automation open. NorthBeam brings perception, mobility, manipulation and hardware design together around one measurable outcome: verified installation per metre.
