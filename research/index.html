Our validation approach

A demo is a beginning.
Evidence is the next step.

Define the conditions

State the environment, task, assumptions and limits before evaluating performance.

Test recovery

Consider interrupted tasks, uncertain perception and how a person takes control.

Measure the work

Evaluate task completion and quality—not just whether the robot moved.

Document the result

Record what was tested, what succeeded and what still needs development.
